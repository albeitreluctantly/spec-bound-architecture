# Specification guide

> [!WARNING]
> Draft outline, not ready for use. Detailed authoring guidance follows version 0.5.

## Feature boundaries

One bounded capability; owned behavior and declared dependencies; shared parent requirements; split/merge review.

## Referencing other features

Handoff, Embedding, Dependency, and Modification expressed through requirements without duplicating another capability's internals.

## Formats and templates

User stories, optional EARS-style criteria, consumer contract tables, and reports derived from other owners' specifications.

## Quality checks

Completeness, consistency, states, crucial alternate outcomes, constraints, and enough information to implement without guessing.

Specification homes and development options are in [Workflows](../05-workflows/workflows.md#spec-driven-development).
